MY SINGING DOGS — Version 1.0
============================
A standalone musical creature-collection browser game with two islands and
sixteen island-dog slots (thirteen unique dog designs).

SETUP (GITHUB PAGES)
--------------------
1. Create a GitHub repository (public for standard GitHub Pages).
2. Upload ALL files from this ZIP DIRECTLY to the repository root.
   Do NOT put the contents in a subfolder.
3. Open repository Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch.
4. Select main, / (root), then Save. Open the URL Pages provides.

PLAY
----
- Tap Enter the islands. Use Play in the bottom music bar to hear your dogs.
- Visit the Market for single-element dogs and treats.
- Breed two DIFFERENT dogs to discover combined-element dogs.
- A Double + Triple pairing can hatch a Mythical (10.5% chance, with a
  guaranteed success by the 9th attempt); unsuccessful attempts produce a
  triple-element egg. Only one egg may incubate on each island at once.
- Hatch at the Nursery, then add the new voice to your island's full song.
- Tap a placed dog to collect coins, feed, mute or change its personal volume.
- Drag a placed dog to reposition it.
- Collect coins for upgrades. Goals award more free resources.
- The Concert button previews all 8 parts of the island's song, including
  parts you have not unlocked yet. Exit preview to hear your own dog choir.
- Use Settings to export/import saves, enable reduced-motion, adjust sound.

THE MUSIC
---------
Plant Island: 112 BPM, 64 bars, 2:17.1 (8 individually synchronized stems).
Ice Island: 96 BPM, 64 bars, 2:40.0 (8 individually synchronized stems).
Both are original musical arrangements, divided into eight sections. The
performers play their own notes, pause, and re-enter as their parts require.
The recordings use synthesized sampled-instrument sounds, not live singing.

SAVES & OFFLINE PLAY
--------------------
Progress is saved locally in the current browser's localStorage; it is not
cloud-synced. To transfer devices or survive clearing browser data, export
your save from Settings and import it on the other device. The service worker
caches the game's visual assets and audio files for offline use after one
complete online load; storage or browser limitations may prevent caching.

QUALITY NOTES
-------------
Desktop and mobile touch interactions are supported. Browser permissions
require the player to tap Play before music can start. Character performance
movements are triggered by their scored notes, but individual jaw/ear rigs
are not fully articulated 3D skeletal animations; this is a browser sprite
game. A sound evaluation on actual headphones is strongly recommended.
